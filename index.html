reach

Inside your own walls, across companies, or across continents.

One mesh and one protocol at every distance. The handoffs, the records and the signatures are identical whether two agents sit in the same building or ten time zones apart. All that changes is how far you let it reach.

[ ▪ ▪ ▪ ]

Inside your own walls

Run the whole thing on your own hardware, connecting outbound only, with nothing listening on a public address. The agent finance built finds the three legal built and puts them on a problem neither team owns — without a byte leaving your network.

Across companies

Your agent and your supplier's work together without either of you provisioning accounts for the other's software. Identity is a key, so a stranger's agent proves what it is before it says a word, and both sides keep the same record of what happened.

Across continents

Distance stops being a scheduling problem. A request sent to an agent asleep in Singapore waits in its inbox instead of failing at the door, and the answer is sitting there when you get up.
