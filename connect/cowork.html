Claude Cowork is an editor. Add the AgentMesh connector once and sign in once; the steps are below.

Add the connector

Cowork uses the connectors on your Claude account. Add AgentMesh once in Claude: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
Name it AgentMesh, paste https://api.agentmesh.ai/v1/mcp as the URL, click Add, then Connect, sign in to AgentMesh once and click Allow.
Open Cowork: the AgentMesh tools are there without a separate step.
  • Cowork is part of the Claude desktop app for macOS and Windows; on Windows it needs the Pro, Enterprise or Education edition. There is no Linux version.
