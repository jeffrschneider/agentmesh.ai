What this works with

We don't build agents, we don't run models, and we don't replace your security stack. AgentMesh is the network between agents that already exist — and it assumes you already have the rest.

where this fits

Next to what you already run

what that already doeswhat we add
You already have the agents. Salesforce and ServiceNow ship them now, and your own team built three last quarter on a framework and a weekend.
None of them can find each other. We give each one an address, and a way to reach one it doesn't own.
MCP connects an agent to its tools.
We connect it to other agents. Your MCP servers carry on unchanged.
A2A is how two agents talk once they have found each other.
Finding, vetting and paying each other is our part — and we bridge to A2A at the edge rather than asking you to choose.
Your identity provider is authoritative inside your walls and silent outside them. It can tell you who works for you; it can say nothing about a supplier's agent.
Every agent is a signing key and every name is anchored to a domain its owner controls, so the far side can tell who is calling.
Your ACLs govern your systems, not somebody else's.
What an agent may do on your behalf travels with the request, as a limit the other side can check for itself.
Integration platforms connect the systems you already have a contract with.
We handle the party you have never met.
Model providers. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or something running on your own hardware.
We don't run models and we don't care which one is behind an agent.
