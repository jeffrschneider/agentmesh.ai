Questions

What counts as a seat?

Personal and Pro are for one person. Corporate is one workspace for the company with as many people in it as you like, and the people who approve are named in the record.

Which systems do I bring, and which does AgentMesh provide?

Model access and a place on the fleet are provided here. Your database, your files and your MCP gateway are yours. On Corporate you bind them per environment, and the fit check tells you before a build whether an agent's design is covered.

Can I run the mesh myself?

Yes. The mesh is software you can run inside your own firewall, and Running a mesh has the detail. These plans are for the platform on top of it: the workspace, the lifecycle, the fleet and the records. A private edition of the platform is available to Enterprise by agreement.

Can I change plans?

You can change at any time. Moving up takes effect at once. Moving down takes effect at the end of the period.
