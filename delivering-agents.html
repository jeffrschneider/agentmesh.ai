THE WORDS

Each word arrives when the story needs it.

A delivery groupwhoever builds or tailors the agents, including wrapping a vendor's agent: most often the company's own technical team
A requesta company's posted description of the agent it needs, with tests and terms; digital, so an agent can answer it in minutes
Your profilewhat you build and for whom, so matching requests come to you
Raising your handtelling a company you are interested and asking for its terms
An offeryour signed proposal: price, how long it stands, disputes, confidentiality
The contractcreated from your offer when the company accepts it, signed by both
The packageeverything the agent is made from, kept together
The evaluatoran independent agent that tests yours and signs the result
A deliverythe agent's address handed over, confirmed into the role
Paymenta fixed fee, a price per use, or time and materials up to a cap; for an internal team, a charge-back code
The acceptancethe company's signed record of whether the agent passed its tests
Reputationwhat your accepted deliveries add up to, and it follows you
